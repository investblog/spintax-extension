Pitch a story

Some forms ask for more than text: a category, a short pitch with a character limit, a logo. Each comes from a column of your list.

The message is longer than 200 characters — the helper refuses to truncate it and tells you so; the topic is picked from the list; the logo comes from the asset library once you add a file named logo.png.
